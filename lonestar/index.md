# Lonestar Conference

Your competition hub — schedules, assignments, reports, and more.

[Go to MatchReadyTX](https://matchreadytx.web.app)

The **Lonestar Conference** runs through **MatchReadyTX**, the platform where men's and women's teams, referees, CMOs, and schedulers manage everything for the season in one place.

View fixtures, raise your hand for games, accept assignments, submit match and coaching reports, confirm matches, and track standings, all from your phone or laptop.



### Players & Teams

Manage your club profile, browse the schedule, confirm match details, and stay connected with your competition.

### Referees & CMOs

Raise your hand for available games, accept assignments, view match details and fees, and submit match and coaching reports when the final whistle blows.

### Schedulers & Admins

Assign crews, manage raise-hand queues, release schedules, and keep the whole conference running smoothly.

MatchReadyTX handles Lonestar match operations — but **all referees must still be registered in Rugby Xplorer (RX)** for certification and official membership with Texas Referees.
